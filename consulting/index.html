01 Common agency challenges

Sound familiar?

01
Market readiness
Foundations aren't solid enough yet. Pushing sales or hiring ahead of that would just amplify the cracks, and waste money.
02
Marketing, sales & delivery aren't joined up
Everything feels disconnected. Delivery teams are busy, but not feeding growth, and there's no shared view of what the agency is pushing towards.
03
No clearly productised offer
There isn't a defined programme with a fixed outcome. It feels open-ended, which makes it harder to buy.
04
Core assets need tightening
Website, copy, case studies, proof points, pillars — they exist, but the positioning isn't landing hard, in a compelling way.
05
No orchestration capacity
There isn't enough time or bandwidth internally to align everything properly and drive it through.
06
Pace not matching ambition
Progress feels slower and lighter than it should be.
07
The pipeline isn't visible or trusted
No clear pipeline stages or definitions, and it's difficult to understand what is currently in play. Forecasting feels like guesswork.
08
Senior cost barriers
Senior growth thinking is required, but can't commit £120k–£150k for a full-time role, especially in the current economic climate.

— Why the need for external support?

Why external
support?

For many agencies, consistent growth is a challenge. Hiring a senior new business lead full-time is expensive, and often unrealistic for smaller or scaling teams.

Internal teams are stretched, with client work taking priority over working on the business. Pipelines become reactive, marketing efforts stall, and growth becomes inconsistent.

Without the time or headspace to focus on strategy, positioning, and installing behaviour change in your process, lead generation gets neglected, and opportunities are missed.

Bringing in an experienced consultant offers a flexible, cost-effective way to inject momentum, structure, and senior thinking — without the overhead of a permanent hire.

— How we'd work together

How we'd work together

1
Step 01
Discovery call

Goal: Understand your goals, gaps and challenges, and whether we're the right fit.

What it involves: A 45–60 min call with the founder or leadership team to discuss ambitions, pipeline performance, marketing activity, historic growth efforts, and expectations of external support.

2
Step 02
Opportunity mapping

Goal: Gather a fuller picture of your situation via a structured questionnaire.

What it involves: You complete a short but focused form. I may request access to key assets (creds deck, outreach emails, pipeline reports).

Outcome: I'll identify the core issues holding growth back and the levers to pull for maximum impact.

3
Step 03
Proposal & plan of action

Goal: A specific engagement plan outlining recommended focus areas and associated actions.

What it involves: A short written proposal with scope options (retainer vs short project), deliverables, and early quick wins — followed by a call to review and align.
